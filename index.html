Privacy Policy – Tag 3D

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tag 3D ("the App") handles information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Tag 3D does not require users to create an account or provide personal information directly to play the game.

However, certain third-party services integrated into the App may automatically collect information when the App is used.

This information may include:

  • Device information and identifiers
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifiers
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Advertising interaction data
  • Diagnostics and crash information

The information collected depends on the services used and the device's privacy settings.

2. Advertising – Google AdMob

Tag 3D uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information, including device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions and diagnostic information, to provide, measure and improve advertising services.

Depending on applicable laws, user consent and the advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google may use collected information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy .

Users can manage certain advertising and privacy preferences through their device settings and Google's available controls.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers, such as Google AdMob, as necessary to provide advertising and related services.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

4. Children's Privacy

Tag 3D is designed for a general audience, primarily teenagers and adults. The App is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

The App uses Google AdMob for advertising. Advertising services may process certain device and usage information as described in this Privacy Policy.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

5. Data Retention

Tag 3D does not operate its own user account database or independently retain personal information submitted by users.

Information collected by third-party services, including Google AdMob, is retained according to those providers' applicable policies and retention practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the App and limit the information processed through our services.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

For information processed by Google, you may also consult Google's privacy controls: Google Privacy Controls .

Users located in the European Economic Area may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
