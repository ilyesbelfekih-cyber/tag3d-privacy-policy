Privacy Policy – Tag 3D

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tag 3D ("the App") handles information when you use our mobile game on Android and iOS.

1. Information We Collect

Tag 3D does not require users to create an account or provide personal information directly to play the game. Your progress (coins, cosmetics, levels, trophies, settings and custom arenas) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us. There is no chat or sharing between players.

However, certain third-party services integrated into the App may automatically collect information when the App is used.

This information may include:

  • Device information and identifiers
  • IP address (used to estimate an approximate location)
  • App usage and interaction data (anonymous analytics)
  • Advertising interaction data
  • Diagnostics and crash information

The information collected depends on the services used and the device's privacy settings.

2. Advertising – Google AdMob

Tag 3D uses Google AdMob to display advertisements:

  • Rewarded ads, shown only when you choose to watch one (to unlock a cosmetic, get coins or double your coins). These buttons are always clearly labeled "AD".
  • Interstitial ads, shown at most once every two matches when you return to the menu, and announced by an "Ad break" message.

Because Tag 3D can be played by children, every ad request is marked as child-directed (COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (GDPR). As a result:

  • ads are never personalized and are not based on your interests;
  • only ads rated for general audiences are shown, and sensitive ad categories are blocked;
  • your device's advertising identifier is not used to target ads.

Google AdMob may process limited technical information, including device information, IP addresses, advertising interactions and diagnostic information, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Google may use collected information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Analytics – Google Firebase Analytics

Tag 3D uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played and to fix problems. For example: screens viewed, matches started and finished, tutorial progress, menu buttons used, and app crashes.

  • This data is anonymous: it is linked to a random app installation ID, not to your name, email address or advertising identifier.
  • Advertising identifier collection and ad personalization signals are disabled in Firebase Analytics.
  • It is never used for advertising, never sold, and only used to improve the game.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security .

4. iOS – App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iPhone and iPad, Tag 3D may request your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.

Whatever you choose, ads in Tag 3D stay non-personalized and Firebase Analytics never uses your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you select "Ask App Not to Track", the app will not access your IDFA.

You can change your tracking preferences at any time in your device settings under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers, namely Google AdMob and Google Firebase, as necessary to provide advertising, analytics and related services on our behalf.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

6. Children's Privacy

Tag 3D is a casual game for a general audience and can be played by children. We comply with Google Play's Families Policy.

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information such as a name, email address, phone number, photo or precise location.

Advertising is configured for children as described in section 2: child-directed requests, non-personalized ads only, general-audience content only. Analytics is anonymous and does not use advertising identifiers, as described in section 3.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention

Tag 3D does not operate its own user account database or independently retain personal information submitted by users. Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device.

Information collected by third-party services, including Google AdMob and Google Firebase Analytics, is retained according to those providers' applicable policies and retention settings.

8. Data Security

All data sent by the App is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We take reasonable measures to protect the App and limit the information processed through our services.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Because the App has no accounts and the data described above is not linked to your identity, we may not be able to identify the data of a specific player.

For information processed by Google, you may also consult Google's privacy controls: Google Privacy Controls .

Users located in the European Economic Area may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
